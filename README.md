# AI Ethics & Moral Decision Analytics

> A data-driven exploration of artificial intelligence ethics, moral decision-making, and philosophical frameworks using statistical analysis, machine learning, and synthetic data.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

**AI Ethics & Moral Decision Analytics** is an interdisciplinary data analytics project that explores how different philosophical frameworks can be applied to ethical decisions made by or involving Artificial Intelligence systems.

The project combines:

- Artificial Intelligence Ethics
- Philosophy
- Data Analytics
- Statistical Analysis
- Machine Learning
- Decision Science
- Responsible AI

A synthetic dataset containing **1,000 simulated AI ethics decision cases** is analyzed across multiple real-world-inspired scenarios.

The objective is not to determine an objectively "correct" moral decision, but to demonstrate how ethical reasoning can be structured, quantified, compared, and analyzed through a data-driven framework.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The major objectives of this project are:

1. Analyze ethical decision-making in AI systems.
2. Compare different philosophical approaches to AI ethics.
3. Study relationships between fairness, privacy, autonomy, transparency, accountability, and utility.
4. Develop analytical ethics indices.
5. Perform statistical and machine learning analysis.
6. Identify patterns and segments within ethical decision cases.
7. Visualize ethical trade-offs.
8. Demonstrate responsible and explainable AI decision analysis.

---
